First call · 15 minutes · Online boxing coaches

Funnel Discovery Cheat Sheet

Don't tell them they need a funnel. Get them to say the gap: where they are now vs. where they want to be, in dollars.

R1Open

0:00–1:00

Set the agenda. Show you're not desperate.

"My goal is to understand how you get clients right now and see if I can help. If I can't, I'll tell you straight. Cool if I ask a few questions?"

R2Situation

1:00–4:00

How their business works today. Keep it short.

  • What do you sell right now, and at what price?
  • How many clients do you have at the moment?
  • Walk me through how someone goes from seeing your post to paying you.
  • Roughly how many DMs or inquiries a month?

R3Problem

4:00–8:00

Dig in. This is the longest round.

  • Of those inquiries, how many become clients?
  • Where do people drop off?
  • How many hours a week do you spend in DMs?

"Tell me more about that." · "How long has that been happening?" · "Roughly how many? 10? 50?"

R4Impact & goal

8:00–11:00

Find the number. Let them do the math.

  • What's your goal: how many clients, or what monthly income?
  • What's stopping you from getting there now?
  • If nothing changes in 6 months, what does that look like?
  • If calls got booked every week without you, what would you do with your time?

R5Process

11:00–13:00

How they'll decide, and how soon.

  • Have you tried anything to fix this? Courses, an agency, doing it yourself?
  • Would you decide on your own, or is anyone else involved?
  • If something worked, are you looking to start soon or later?

R6Close

13:00–15:00

Summarize, then book the follow-up.

"You're getting [X] inquiries but converting [Y] because [reason]. You want [goal], about [$ gap] a month. And you're spending [Z] hours in DMs. Did I miss anything?"

"I'll put together a quick plan of what your funnel would look like and walk you through it. 20 minutes, [day]?"

"Do you have results?"

"Not yet, and I'll be straight with you. You'd be one of my first boxing coaches. That's why I'm offering a founding rate, and [you pay the rest once your funnel is live]. In return I'd ask for an honest testimonial if it works."

Guarantee your work, never their results.

Never

  • Pitch the moment they name a problem
  • Accept a vague number
  • Fill a silence after your question
  • Talk funnels more than their clients, income and time
  • Promise a number of clients
